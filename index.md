## About Me

I received my B.Sc. in Computing Science from the [University of Alberta](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/index.html) and my M.A.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca/). I am currently an ML Engineer at [RBC Borealis](https://rbcborealis.com/) (formerly Borealis AI). Prior to that, I worked as a Research Engineer at an AI startup [Leap Tools](https://www.leaptools.com/), where I focused on post-training for MMDiT. At Borealis, my scope is broader: in addition to advancing AI research in Finance, I also help deliver end-to-end ML systems, including ETL, training, inference, and serving.
